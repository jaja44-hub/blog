# Understanding Employee Termination and Severance Pay Under Ethiopian Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019: Complete Guide

## Executive Summary

🔴 **Regulatory Compliance in Ethiopian Employment Relations**: Managing employment contracts, terminations, and employee compensation in Ethiopia requires strict adherence to **Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019**, which repealed the former Labour Proclamation No. 377/2003. Operating an enterprise in Ethiopia—whether a domestic startup, a private limited company (PLC), or a foreign direct investment (FDI) project—demands a comprehensive understanding of statutory worker protections, mandatory severance pay formulas, working hour limitations, and maternity leave rights. Employers who fail to align their human resource procedures with statutory mandates face substantial financial liabilities, compulsory employee reinstatement orders, and administrative penalties from the **Ministry of Labour and Skills (MoLS)**.

🎯 **Key Takeaways for HR Managers & Employers**:
- **Statutory Termination Grounds**: Under **Article 27**, summary dismissal without notice is limited strictly to eleven exhaustive legal grounds. Termination for reasons outside these provisions constitutes unlawful dismissal under **Article 43**.
- **Severance Pay Entitlements**: Employees terminated due to redundancy, enterprise closure, or employer breach under **Article 39** are entitled to a base severance package equal to **30 days' average salary** for the first year of service, plus **1/3 of a month's salary** for each subsequent year.
- **Probationary Period Limitations**: Pursuant to **Article 11(3)**, the maximum statutory probation period is **60 working days**, during which either party may terminate the employment contract without notice or severance pay.
- **Maternity Protection Mandate**: Female employees are entitled to **120 consecutive days** of fully paid maternity leave (30 days prenatal and 90 days postnatal) under **Article 88**.

💡 **Target Audience**: This authoritative legal guide is designed for corporate executives, human resource managers, legal counsel, startup founders, and employees seeking comprehensive, source-grounded clarity on Ethiopian labour law compliance in 2026.

## Legal Framework Overview

⚖️ **Governing Legislation and Statutory Mandates**: Employment contracts, working conditions, and termination protocols across Ethiopia's private sector are regulated by **Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019**, published in the Federal Negarit Gazette No. 89/2019. The Proclamation establishes statutory minimum standards that cannot be derogated from to the detriment of employees, even by mutual agreement or individual employment contract, pursuant to **Article 4**.

Key statutory provisions governing employment relations include:
- **Articles 9–11**: Formation and duration of employment contracts (Distinction between contracts of indefinite duration and definite/piece-work contracts under Article 10).
- **Article 11(3)**: Probation period rules, setting a strict statutory ceiling of **60 working days**.
- **Article 26 & 27**: Termination of employment contracts with notice (Article 28) vs. summary dismissal without notice for cause (Article 27).
- **Articles 29–30**: Mass reduction of workforce (redundancy protocols) and criteria for employee retention.
- **Articles 39–41**: Statutory severance pay calculations, conditions, and special compensation for unlawful termination.
- **Articles 61–68**: Statutory working hours (maximum **8 hours per day / 48 hours per week**) and overtime compensation rates (Articles 66–68).
- **Articles 87–88**: Protection of female employees, working condition safeguards, and mandatory **120-day paid maternity leave**.

🏛️ **Regulatory Bodies and Institutional Oversight**:
1. **Ministry of Labour and Skills (MoLS)**: Exercises federal regulatory oversight over labor relations, inspecting workplaces, registering collective agreements, and issuing workplace health and safety directives.
2. **Regional Labour and Social Affairs Bureaus**: Oversee local labor compliance, handle trade union registrations, and receive mandatory layoff notifications under Article 30.
3. **Labour Relations Boards**: Administrative dispute resolution tribunals established under **Article 147** with jurisdiction over collective labor disputes, conciliation, and lawful strike approvals.

⏱️ **Recent Statutory Interpretations and Compliance Requirements**:
Under 2026 enforcement directives issued by MoLS, employers are subject to heightened scrutiny regarding probationary contract extensions and severance settlement timelines. Labour inspectors strictly enforce the rule that probationary periods exceeding 60 working days automatically convert the worker's status into a permanent employment contract of indefinite duration from day one. Furthermore, final settlement payments upon contract termination must be disbursed within **seven working days** pursuant to **Article 44**, failing which employers incur statutory interest penalties.

### Step 1: Contract Formation and Probationary Period Management
⏱️ **Timeline**: Initial 60 Working Days | 💰 **Cost**: Standard Contracted Wage | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Articles 9, 10, & 11

🔴 **Critical Requirement**: Employment contracts are deemed to be for an indefinite duration unless the work falls strictly under the exceptional temporary categories listed in **Article 10** (e.g., seasonal work, temporary replacement, or specific piece-rate tasks).
- **Procedure**: Draft written employment contracts detailing job description, wage, place of work, and probation terms.
- **Probation Rules**: Under **Article 11(3)**, the probation period must not exceed **60 working days**. The worker must be provided with written confirmation of probation completion or termination prior to the expiration of the 60th working day.
- **Outcome**: Formal classification of permanent worker status upon passing probation without automatic extension rights.

---

### Step 2: Working Hours, Rest Periods, and Overtime Administration
⏱️ **Timeline**: Daily/Weekly Operations | 💰 **Cost**: Standard Wage + Overtime Multipliers | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Articles 61–68

📊 **Statutory Limits**:
- **Normal Working Hours**: Maximum **8 hours per day** or **48 hours per week** (**Article 61**).
- **Weekly Rest**: Minimum **24 consecutive hours** of rest per week, typically falling on Sunday (**Article 69**).
- **Overtime Limits**: Overtime work must not exceed **4 hours in a day** or **12 hours in a week** (**Article 66**).

💰 **Overtime Compensation Multipliers (Article 68)**:
1. **Daytime Overtime (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM)**: Paid at **1.5 times (150%)** the regular hourly rate.
2. **Nighttime Overtime (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM)**: Paid at **1.75 times (175%)** the regular hourly rate.
3. **Weekly Rest Day Overtime**: Paid at **2.0 times (200%)** the regular hourly rate.
4. **Public Holiday Overtime**: Paid at **2.5 times (250%)** the regular hourly rate.

---

### Step 3: Administration of Maternity Leave and Women's Working Protections
⏱️ **Timeline**: 120 Consecutive Days | 💰 **Cost**: 100% Fully Paid by Employer | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Articles 87 & 88

✅ **Best Practice**: Employers must establish clear medical certificate submission workflows to ensure seamless pre- and post-natal leave transitions.
- **Maternity Leave Allocation**: Pregnant employees are entitled to **30 consecutive days** of prenatal leave prior to confinement and **90 consecutive days** of postnatal leave after delivery (**Article 88(3)**), totaling **120 fully paid days**.
- **Prohibited Work**: Under **Article 87**, pregnant women must not be assigned to night work (between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM) or assigned to overtime work.
- **Dismissal Prohibition**: It is strictly illegal under **Article 87(5)** to terminate the employment contract of a woman during her pregnancy or within four months following her delivery.

---

### Step 4: Disciplinary Procedures and Summary Dismissal Without Notice
⏱️ **Timeline**: 30 Calendar Days from Knowledge | 💰 **Cost**: Administrative Costs | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Article 27

🔴 **Critical Requirement**: Employers can only terminate a contract without notice under **Article 27(1)** if the worker commits one of eleven explicit statutory infractions (e.g., repeated unexcused absence for 5 total days in a month, gross negligence, deceitful presentation of credentials, or physical violence at the workplace).
- **Procedural Rules**: The employer must issue written termination notices stating the specific legal grounds and factual reasons within **30 calendar days** of becoming aware of the infraction. Failure to issue notice within 30 days waives the employer's right to summary dismissal.
- **Outcome**: Immediate contract termination without notice or severance pay if statutory grounds are satisfied.

---

### Step 5: Workforce Reduction and Redundancy Protocols
⏱️ **Timeline**: 30 Days Prior Notice | 💰 **Cost**: Full Severance Package + Notice Pay | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Articles 28, 29, & 30

🟡 **Caution**: Reducing headcount due to organizational restructure, technological changes, or economic distress requires strict adherence to statutory selection order under **Article 29(3)**.
- **Mandatory Selection Order**: Retain workers with higher skill and productivity first. If workers have equal skill, retain based on: (1) longer length of service, (2) fewer dependents, (3) non-disabled workers, (4) pregnant women or mothers, and (5) trade union representatives.
- **MoLS Notification**: Written notice detailing the reduction plan must be submitted to the Ministry of Labour and Skills or the local bureau at least **30 calendar days** prior to termination (**Article 30**).

---

### Step 6: Calculation and Disbursement of Severance Pay & Compensation
⏱️ **Timeline**: Settlement Calculation | 💰 **Cost**: 30 Days' Salary + 1/3 Salary per Additional Year | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Articles 39, 40, & 41

⚖️ **Severance Eligibility (Article 39)**: Workers who have completed probation are entitled to statutory severance pay if terminated due to: (1) enterprise closure/bankruptcy, (2) redundancy reduction, (3) employer breach/unlawful conduct, or (4) incapacity/disability.
- **Calculation Formula**:
  - **First Year of Service**: **30 days' average salary** (1 full month's salary).
  - **Subsequent Years of Service**: **1/3 of a month's salary** for each additional year of service.
  - **Pro-Rata Application**: For service fractions below a full year, severance is calculated proportionally.
  - **Statutory Ceiling**: Total severance pay cannot exceed **12 months' salary** (**Article 40(2)**).
- **Special Redundancy Add-On (Article 40(3))**: Employees terminated due to workforce reduction receive an additional **60 days' average salary** on top of the base severance calculation.

---

### Step 7: Final Settlement Disbursals and Service Certificate Issuance
⏱️ **Timeline**: Within 7 Working Days | 💰 **Cost**: Accumulated Wages & Severance | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Articles 44 & 123

✅ **Best Practice**: Provide employees with a signed certificate of service immediately upon contract end to prevent post-employment administrative disputes.
- **Payment Deadline**: Under **Article 44**, all final payments—including unpaid wages, accrued unused annual leave, notice pay, and severance—must be paid within **7 working days** of contract termination.
- **Certificate of Service**: Pursuant to **Article 123**, the employer must issue a free certificate stating the type of work performed, duration of service, and salary paid, without making negative comments regarding worker conduct.

### Mathematical Formulas: Severance Pay & Overtime Rate Calculations

**Formula 1: Base Severance Pay Calculation**
Under **Article 40(1)**, base severance pay for an employee who has completed probation is calculated using the employee's average daily wage over the preceding 3 months:

$$ \text{Base Severance Pay} = \text{Monthly Salary} + \left( \frac{\text{Monthly Salary}}{3} \times (\text{Years of Service} - 1) \right) $$

*Step-by-Step Calculation Example*:
1. **Employee Monthly Salary**: 18,000 ETB
2. **Total Completed Length of Service**: 4 Years
3. **First Year Base Entitlement**: $18,000 \text{ ETB}$ (Equivalent to 30 days' salary)
4. **Subsequent 3 Years Calculation**:
$$ \text{Subsequent Service Allowance} = 3 \times \left( \frac{18,000}{3} \right) = 3 \times 6,000 = 18,000 \text{ ETB} $$
5. **Total Base Severance Pay**:
$$ \text{Total Severance} = 18,000 + 18,000 = 36,000 \text{ ETB} $$

**Formula 2: Redundancy Termination Package Calculation (Including Add-On)**
Under **Article 40(3)**, employees terminated due to workforce reduction receive the base severance pay plus an additional **60 days' average salary**:

$$ \text{Total Redundancy Settlement} = \text{Base Severance Pay} + (2 \times \text{Monthly Salary}) $$

*Step-by-Step Calculation Example*:
1. **Base Severance Pay (from Formula 1)**: 36,000 ETB
2. **Monthly Salary**: 18,000 ETB
3. **60-Day Redundancy Add-On**: $2 \times 18,000 = 36,000 \text{ ETB}$
4. **Total Package**:
$$ \text{Total Redundancy Settlement} = 36,000 + 36,000 = 72,000 \text{ ETB} $$

Misinterpreting Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019 exposes business owners and corporate managers to statutory litigation, mandatory back-pay orders, and labor board sanctions.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **❌ Extending Probation Beyond 60 Days** | Contract automatically converts into permanent indefinite employment; termination becomes unlawful dismissal | ✅ Limit written probation clauses to <= 60 working days and evaluate performance prior to day 60 | Labour Proclamation 1156/2019 Art. 11(3) |
| **❌ Unlawful Summary Dismissal Without Cause** | Labour Board orders employee reinstatement with full back-pay or up to 180 days compensation | ✅ Ensure dismissals without notice strictly match one of the 11 grounds in Article 27 and issue notice within 30 days | Labour Proclamation 1156/2019 Art. 27 & 43 |

| **❌ Miscalculating Overtime Rates on Weekends** | Wage underpayment claims; employee labor grievances; accumulated back-pay liabilities | ✅ Apply 2.0x multiplier for Sunday rest day overtime and 2.5x multiplier for public holidays | Labour Proclamation 1156/2019 Art. 68 |
| **❌ Delaying Final Settlement Beyond 7 Days** | Accumulation of statutory interest penalties and legal fees upon Labour Board filing | ✅ Disburse final wages, unused annual leave, and severance within 7 working days of contract end | Labour Proclamation 1156/2019 Art. 44 |
