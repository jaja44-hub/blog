# How to Register a Business under the New Ethiopian Commercial Code: A Complete Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

## Executive Summary

🔴 **Navigating Commercial Compliance in Ethiopia**: Navigating the formal entry requirements into the Ethiopian marketplace requires a detailed understanding of the statutory framework established under the new Commercial Code of Ethiopia (Proclamation No. 1243/2021)[^1]. Following comprehensive structural modernization and full digital integration through the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (MoTRI) e-Trade portal, business registration in 2026 has transitioned from fragmented paper-based bureau bureaucracy into an integrated electronic system. However, legal entities and individual entrepreneurs must strictly satisfy statutory prerequisites before commencing commercial operations.

🎯 **Key Takeaways for Investors & Entrepreneurs**:
- **Statutory Framework**: Commercial registration is mandatory under Article 83 of Proclamation No. 1243/2021 before engaging in commercial activities.
- **Digitized Execution**: Step-by-step registration is routed through the centralized e-Trade database and tax portal, linking MoTRI, Ministry of Revenue (MoR), and the Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC).
- **Capital Thresholds**: Sole proprietorships and Limited Liability Companies (PLCs) no longer face a statutory minimum capital baseline under the Commercial Code, whereas Share Companies require a minimum capital of 50,000 ETB[^2], and foreign investors face specific statutory capital thresholds under Proclamation No. 1180/2020[^3].
- **Authentication Mandate**: Physical lease agreements and corporate Articles of Association require mandatory verification by the Documents Authentication and Registration Service (DARS).

💡 **Target Audience**: This comprehensive guide provides step-by-step legal guidance for domestic corporate entities, foreign direct investors, tech startups, legal practitioners, and business consultants seeking full operational compliance in Addis Ababa and across Ethiopia.

## Legal Framework Overview

⚖️ **Governing Legislation and Statutory Mandates**: Commercial entity formation and trade licensing in Ethiopia are primarily governed by the **Commercial Code of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1243/2021**[^1], which repealed Book One, Two, and Three of the historic 1960 Commercial Code. The 2021 Code modernized corporate structures by introducing novel legal entities, such as the One-Person Private Limited Company (Article 172(3))[^4] and Limited Liability Partnerships (Article 221)[^5], while streamlining corporate governance and registration protocols.

Key statutory provisions governing business establishment include:
- **Article 5**: Statutory definition of traders carrying out commercial activities for gain[^6].
- **Article 70 & 72**: Establishment of the Federal Commercial Register under MoTRI and the Central Commercial Registration Database[^7].
- **Article 82 & 83**: Mandatory commercial registration requirements prior to trade activity[^8].
- **Article 174**: Recognized forms of business organizations (General Partnership, Limited Partnership, Limited Liability Partnership, Joint Venture, Share Company, Private Limited Company, and One-Person PLC)[^9].
- **Article 247 & 254**: Capital subscription and bank account block deposit requirements for Share Companies[^10].

🏛️ **Regulatory Authorities and Institutional Roles**:
1. **Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (MoTRI)**: Serves as the central regulatory authority responsible for administering the Federal Commercial Register, issuing nationwide trade name clearances, approving business licenses, and maintaining the automated e-Trade database[^7].
2. **Ministry of Revenue (MoR)**: Oversees Tax Identification Number (TIN) issuance, Value Added Tax (VAT) registration, and regional tax compliance.
3. **Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC)**: Exercises exclusive regulatory jurisdiction over foreign direct investment (FDI), issuing foreign investment permits, and validating minimum foreign capital deposits pursuant to Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020[^3].
4. **Documents Authentication and Registration Service (DARS)**: Authenticates lease agreements, powers of attorney, and Memorandum / Articles of Association.

⏱️ **Recent Regulatory and Digital Modernization Updates**:
In 2026, MoTRI completed full system integration between the e-Trade portal, National ID (Fayda) database, and the Ministry of Revenue's digital tax portal. The manual submission of physical documents at regional trade bureaus has been systematically replaced by digital verification, reducing name clearance and commercial registration timelines from weeks to mere business days. Furthermore, commercial registration must now be strictly synchronized with annual business license renewal schedules to prevent statutory cancellation under Article 96[^11].

## Step-by-Step Procedures

Executing business registration under the Ethiopian Commercial Code requires navigating seven sequential procedural stages. Entrepreneurs must complete each prerequisite step prior to moving forward.

### Step 1: Name Clearance & Verification (MoTRI / e-Trade Portal)
⏱️ **Timeline**: 1–2 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: ~200 ETB Application Fee | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Commercial Code Article 176[^12]

🔴 **Critical Requirement**: Every business entity must adopt a distinct trade name that does not infringe upon existing registered trademarks or trade names, nor contravene public morality.
- **Procedure**: Submit three prospective trade names through the MoTRI e-Trade portal or at an authorized regional trade bureau.
- **Documentation**: Applicant National ID (Fayda) or Passport, proposed name list in Amharic and English.
- **Outcome**: MoTRI issues a digital **Trade Name Clearance Certificate** valid for 60 calendar days.

---

### Step 2: Tax Identification Number (TIN) Registration
⏱️ **Timeline**: 1–2 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: Free of Charge | ⚖️ **Governing Authority**: Ministry of Revenue (MoR)

✅ **Best Practice**: Individual founders must hold a personal TIN prior to registering a corporate Tax Identification Number for a business entity.
- **Procedure**: Applicants register biometric data and verify National ID details at the local Ministry of Revenue branch or via the integrated e-Tax portal.
- **Documentation**: Trade Name Clearance Certificate, Fayda National ID, passport-sized digital photographs, and manager appointment letter.
- **Outcome**: A unique 10-digit tax identification number and digital TIN certificate are issued.

---

### Step 3: Lease Agreement Authentication
⏱️ **Timeline**: 1–3 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: ~500–1,500 ETB (Stamp duty & service fees) | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Commercial Code Article 109 & 116[^13]

🟡 **Caution**: Unauthenticated commercial leases are legally unenforceable against third parties and will be rejected during business license application.
- **Procedure**: The property lessor and lessee must present the commercial lease contract at the local Documents Authentication and Registration Service (DARS) branch or Sub-City Land Management office.
- **Documentation**: Original title deed of the property, valid National ID of lessor and lessee, site location map, and structural usage permit.
- **Outcome**: Officially stamped and authenticated commercial lease agreement required for physical location verification.

---

### Step 4: Drafting & Authentication of Memorandum of Association
⏱️ **Timeline**: 2–3 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: ~1,000–3,000 ETB | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Commercial Code Articles 173, 185, & 255[^14]

🔴 **Critical Requirement**: Under Article 17(1) of the Commercial Code, the formation of a business organization is legally void unless established through an authenticated Memorandum of Association[^15].
- **Procedure**: Draft the Memorandum of Association utilizing MoTRI standard templates or customized legal counsel drafts. Submit the document to DARS for formal notary execution.
- **Documentation**: Authenticated lease agreement, TIN certificates of founders, Trade Name Clearance Certificate, and capital allocation schedule.
- **Outcome**: Authenticated Memorandum of Association conferring legal formation rights.

---

### Step 5: Capital Bank Account Opening & Capital Deposit
⏱️ **Timeline**: 1–2 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: Bank service charges (~500 ETB) | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Commercial Code Article 254 & Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020[^3]

📊 **Capital Standards**:
- **Domestic Sole Proprietorships & PLCs**: No statutory minimum capital threshold mandated by the 2021 Commercial Code; founders declare operational capital based on sector requirements.
- **Domestic Share Companies**: Minimum statutory capital of 50,000 ETB, with at least 25% (12,500 ETB) deposited in a blocked bank account prior to registration[^2].
- **Foreign Direct Investors**: Minimum capital of **$200,000 USD** for single wholly foreign-owned investment projects, or **$150,000 USD** for joint investment projects with domestic partners[^3].
- **Procedure**: Present the authenticated Memorandum of Association and MoTRI bank opening letter to a commercial bank to open a "Company Under Formation" blocked bank account.
- **Outcome**: Official Bank Confirmation Letter attesting to capital deposit.

---

### Step 6: Commercial Registration & Business License Issuance
⏱️ **Timeline**: 2–4 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: ~1,000–5,000 ETB (Variable by sector & entity type) | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Commercial Code Article 83[^8]

✅ **Best Practice**: Consolidate all digital clearance tokens onto the e-Trade portal to expedite final license issuance.
- **Procedure**: Upload the Bank Confirmation Letter, DARS-authenticated Memorandum of Association, authenticated lease contract, and TIN to the MoTRI e-Trade portal (or EIC portal for foreign investments).
- **Documentation**: All cumulative certificates from Steps 1–5, health/safety certificates (for specific industrial/catering sectors), and general manager competence credentials.
- **Outcome**: Issuance of the official **Commercial Registration Certificate** and operational **Business License**.

---

### Step 7: Post-Registration Tax Register & Annual License Renewal
⏱️ **Timeline**: Ongoing / Annual | 💰 **Cost**: Annual trade renewal fee (~1,000–3,000 ETB) | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Commercial Code Article 96[^11]

🟡 **Caution**: Business licenses in Ethiopia must be renewed annually between **July 8 and January 7** (Hamle 1 to Tahsas 30 Ethiopian Calendar) following tax clearance. Failure to renew triggers escalating statutory penalties and risk of commercial register cancellation under Article 96.

### Mathematical Formula: Foreign Minimum Capital & License Late Renewal Penalty Calculations

**Formula 1: Minimum Foreign Equity Capital Requirement Calculation**
For foreign investors forming a joint venture with a local partner, the foreign capital input must satisfy statutory dollar-equivalent conversion baselines established by the Ethiopian Investment Commission:

$$ \text{Total Required Capital (ETB)} = \text{Minimum Statutory USD Requirement} \times \text{NBE Official Exchange Rate} $$

*Step-by-Step Calculation Example*:
1. **Statutory Foreign Capital Minimum (Joint Venture)**: $150,000 USD
2. **National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) Official Exchange Rate**: 120 ETB per 1 USD
3. **Mathematical Application**:
$$ \text{Total Capital Deposit} = 150,000 \times 120 = 18,000,000 \text{ ETB} $$
4. **Mandatory Deposit**: The foreign investor must transfer $18,000,000 \text{ ETB}$ equivalent via official banking channels before commercial permit issuance.

**Formula 2: Statutory Business License Late Renewal Fine Structure**
Under Ministry of Trade regulations, failing to renew a business license within the statutory renewal window (Hamle 1 to Tahsas 30 / July 8 to January 7) incurs compounding monthly administrative fines:

$$ \text{Total Late Renewal Penalty} = \text{Base Renewal Fee} + \left( \text{Base Renewal Fee} \times 0.20 \times \text{Months Overdue} \right) $$

*Step-by-Step Calculation Example*:
1. **Base Renewal Fee**: 2,000 ETB
2. **Months Overdue**: 3 Months (e.g., Renewing in Megabit / March)
3. **Calculation**:
$$ \text{Penalty} = 2,000 + \left( 2,000 \times 0.20 \times 3 \right) = 2,000 + 1,200 = 3,200 \text{ ETB} $$
