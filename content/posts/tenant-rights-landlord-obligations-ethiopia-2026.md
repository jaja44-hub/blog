# Tenant Rights and Landlord Obligations in Ethiopia: Complete Legal Guide (2026)

## Executive Summary

🔴 **Navigating Residential and Commercial Tenancy Regulations**: Urban tenancy relations across Ethiopia—particularly within the high-density residential and commercial sectors of Addis Ababa—are governed by a dual regulatory framework comprising the historic **Civil Code Proclamation No. 165/1960** and the modernized **Rent Control and Tenancy Proclamation No. 1313/2024** (complemented by **Real Estate Development and Property Management Proclamation No. 1320/2024**). As urban housing demand expands and commercial lease markets evolve, balancing landlord property returns against statutory tenant protections has become a critical focal point of municipal policy. Landlords and tenants must navigate strict statutory rules regarding lease contract authentication, security deposit caps, annual rent increase restrictions, maintenance liability allocations, and judicial eviction restrictions.

🎯 **Key Takeaways for Landlords & Tenants**:
- **Statutory Framework**: General lease contracts are grounded in **Civil Code Articles 2896–2974**, while urban residential tenancies are subject to mandatory rent control and eviction caps under **Proclamation No. 1313/2024**.
- **Mandatory Lease Authentication**: Under **Civil Code Article 1723** and municipal administrative directives, commercial and residential leases exceeding statutory threshold durations must be authenticated before the **Documents Authentication and Registration Service (DARS)** and registered with the municipal Sub-City Rent Registry.
- **Rent Increase Caps**: **Proclamation No. 1313/2024** restricts arbitrary mid-term rent increases, establishing that residential rents may only be adjusted once per year in accordance with municipal inflation caps published by the **Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure (MUI)**.
- **Eviction Protection**: Landlords cannot execute summary or arbitrary evictions; lawful lease termination requires serving written statutory notice (**1 to 3 months** under Civil Code Article 2957) and securing a valid eviction order from the **Municipal Tenancy Dispute Tribunal**.

💡 **Target Audience**: This comprehensive legal guide provides practical strategies for residential tenants, commercial business lessees, property owners, real estate managers, and legal practitioners seeking full compliance guidance in 2026.

## Legal Framework Overview

⚖️ **Governing Legislation and Statutory Mandates**: Tenancy agreements, property maintenance obligations, rent control measures, and eviction procedures in Ethiopia are regulated by federal proclamations, civil code provisions, and municipal directives:

1. **Civil Code of the Empire of Ethiopia (Proclamation No. 165/1960)**: Book IV, Title XVI, Chapter 2 (Articles 2896–2974) provides the foundational private law governing the lease of immovable property, defining essential lease terms, delivery obligations, repair liabilities, and termination mechanisms.
2. **Urban Residential Rent Control and Tenancy Proclamation No. 1313/2024**: Federal legislation establishing statutory rent caps, prohibiting arbitrary price gouging, mandating written tenancy registration, and establishing specialized Municipal Tenancy Dispute Tribunals.
3. **Real Estate Development and Property Management Proclamation No. 1320/2024**: Regulates commercial lease management, property management service standards, common area maintenance cost allocations, and condominium owner-tenant associations.
4. **Civil Procedure Code of Ethiopia (Proclamation No. 52/1965)**: Governs judicial eviction proceedings, execution of court possession orders, and summary procedure suits for uncollected rent arrears.
5. **Addis Ababa City Administration Tenancy Directive No. 04/2026**: Establishes municipal lease registration workflows, sub-city rent ceiling benchmarks, and administrative dispute resolution procedures across all 11 sub-cities.

Key statutory provisions governing tenancy rights include:
- **Civil Code Article 2898 & 2900**: Mandatory obligation of the landlord to deliver the leased property in a good state of repair and fit for the intended residential or commercial purpose.
- **Civil Code Article 2916 & 2917**: Allocation of maintenance responsibilities: major structural repairs remain the exclusive liability of the landlord, while routine minor maintenance (wear and tear) falls upon the tenant.
- **Proclamation 1313/2024, Article 6**: Mandatory written registration of all urban residential lease agreements with the Sub-City Land and Rent Administration Bureau.
- **Proclamation 1313/2024, Article 11**: Strict prohibition against arbitrary mid-lease eviction or unilateral rent increases exceeding published municipal annual caps.
- **Civil Code Article 2957**: Statutory notice periods for lease termination of contracts executed for an indefinite duration (minimum 3 months' notice for residential property; 1 month for commercial premises).

🏛️ **Regulatory Authorities and Institutional Oversight**:
1. **Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure (MUI)**: Establishes national urban housing policies, sets benchmark rent increase guidelines, and coordinates municipal tenancy registries.
2. **Addis Ababa City Administration Rent Control and Tenancy Registry Bureau**: Registers residential and commercial lease contracts, maintains the municipal rental database, and issues compliance tokens.
3. **Municipal Tenancy Dispute Tribunals**: Specialized administrative courts possessing exclusive primary jurisdiction over landlord-tenant disputes, rent adjustment grievances, and eviction applications.
4. **Documents Authentication and Registration Service (DARS)**: Public notary authority responsible for authenticating long-term residential and commercial lease agreements.

⏱️ **Recent Regulatory Updates and Municipal Enforcement**:
In 2026, the Addis Ababa City Administration enforced mandatory digital registration for all urban residential leases through the **e-Tenancy Portal**. Under the updated framework, uncertified verbal lease contracts cannot be enforced before municipal tribunals, and landlords who collect unrecorded rent face administrative fines equal to three months' rent value under **Proclamation No. 1313/2024**.

### Step 1: Pre-Occupancy Property Inspection and Condition Inventory
⏱️ **Timeline**: Pre-Move-In | 💰 **Cost**: Free of Charge | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Civil Code Article 2900

🔴 **Critical Requirement**: Under **Civil Code Article 2900**, in the absence of a written inventory showing the state of the premises at move-in, the tenant is legally presumed to have received the property in good condition.
- **Procedure**: Landlord and tenant must jointly inspect the premises prior to handing over keys.
- **Documentation**: Draft a written **Property Condition Report** detailing electrical fixtures, plumbing, wall paint, doors, windows, and existing utility meter readings (water and electricity).
- **Outcome**: Signed pre-occupancy inventory preventing post-lease security deposit disputes.

---

### Step 2: Drafting the Tenancy Contract
⏱️ **Timeline**: 1–2 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: Standard Legal Drafting Fee | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Civil Code Articles 2896–2905 & Proclamation 1313/2024

✅ **Best Practice**: Include clear statutory clauses defining the lease duration, exact rent payment date, permitted property usage (residential vs. commercial), and utility cost allocations.
- **Mandatory Terms Required**:
  - Full legal names, Fayda National IDs, and contact addresses of landlord and tenant.
  - Precise description and physical address of the leased property.
  - Agreed monthly rent amount and statutory rent review terms.
  - Security deposit value and explicit refund criteria.
  - Duration of lease (fixed term vs. indefinite contract).

---

### Step 3: DARS Notary Authentication and Municipal e-Tenancy Registration
⏱️ **Timeline**: 2–3 Business Days | 💰 **Cost**: ~500–1,500 ETB Notary & Registration Fees | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Civil Code Article 1723 & Proclamation 1313/2024 Art. 6

🔴 **Critical Requirement**: All urban residential tenancy contracts must be registered with the municipal **Sub-City Rent Control Registry**, and long-term commercial leases must be authenticated before **DARS**.
- **Procedure**: Present signed lease contracts, property title deed, and Fayda National IDs at DARS or upload the digital contract via the municipal **e-Tenancy portal**.
- **Outcome**: Issuance of a certified **Tenancy Registration Token**, conferring legal standing before municipal tribunals.

---

### Step 4: Administration of Security Deposits
⏱️ **Timeline**: Upon Contract Execution | 💰 **Cost**: Max 2 Months' Rent | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Proclamation 1313/2024 Article 9

📊 **Statutory Caps**:
- **Residential Security Deposit Ceiling**: Security deposits for urban residential leases are capped at a maximum of **two (2) months' rent**.
- **Refund Terms**: The deposit must be refunded to the tenant within **15 calendar days** of lease termination, minus documented costs for actual tenant-caused damage or unpaid utility bills. Normal wear and tear cannot be deducted.

---

### Step 5: Monthly Rent Collection and Income Tax Filing
⏱️ **Timeline**: Monthly / Annual | 💰 **Cost**: Progressive Tax Rates (10%–35%) | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Income Tax Proclamation 979/2016 Article 13

💰 **Landlord Tax Liabilities**: Landlords leasing real estate must issue written receipts for all rent payments and file annual **Rental Income Tax** declarations with the Ministry of Revenue or regional tax bureau.
- **Tax Brackets**: Individual landlords are taxed on a progressive scale ranging from **10% to 35%** on net rental income after standard deductions, while corporate landlords pay a flat **30% rate**.

---

### Step 6: Allocation of Maintenance Responsibilities
⏱️ **Timeline**: Throughout Tenancy | 💰 **Cost**: Variable | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Civil Code Articles 2916 & 2917

⚖️ **Division of Maintenance Obligations**:
- **Landlord Responsibility (Article 2916)**: Major structural repairs, roof leakages, external wall integrity, main plumbing lines, electrical wiring infrastructure, and inherent structural defects.
- **Tenant Responsibility (Article 2917)**: Routine minor maintenance resulting from daily usage, lightbulb replacements, internal faucet washer changes, and repairing damage caused by tenant fault or negligence.

---

### Step 7: Lawful Lease Termination and Eviction Procedures
⏱️ **Timeline**: 1–3 Months Notice | 💰 **Cost**: Court Filing Fees | ⚖️ **Governing Provision**: Civil Code Article 2957 & Proclamation 1313/2024

🟡 **Caution**: Self-help evictions—such as changing door locks, cutting off electricity or water utilities, or forcibly ejecting a tenant—are strictly illegal and constitute criminal offences under the Criminal Code.
- **Lawful Eviction Steps**:
  1. Issue a formal written **Notice to Quit** stating legal grounds and respecting statutory notice periods (minimum **3 months** for residential; **1 month** for commercial).
  2. If the tenant fails to vacate upon notice expiration, file an eviction petition before the **Municipal Tenancy Dispute Tribunal**.
  3. Execute the tribunal's formal possession order through official court bailiffs.

Under **Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016, Article 13**, rental income earned by individual landlords is subject to progressive tax brackets after deducting statutory allowances. 1. **Gross Monthly Rent Collected**: 20,000 ETB

4. **Net Taxable Rental Income**: (240,000 - 72,000 = 168,000 	ext{ ETB})
5. **Applicable Progressive Tax Bracket (168,000 ETB falls in the 30% Bracket for 108,001–180,000 ETB with 14,700 ETB Constant)**:
\[	ext{Tax Payable} = (168,000 	imes 0.30) - 14,700 = 50,400 - 14,700 = 35,700 	ext{ ETB/Year}$$

Under **Proclamation No. 1313/2024**, the landlord must calculate the net security deposit refund upon lease end:

\[	ext{Net Refund Payable} = 	ext{Initial Security Deposit} - \left( 	ext{Documented Tenant Damage Repairs} + 	ext{Unpaid Utility Arrears} 
ight)$$

*Step-by-Step Calculation Example*:

\[	ext{Refund Amount} = 30,000 - (3,500 + 1,200) = 30,000 - 4,700 = 25,300 	ext{ ETB}$$
