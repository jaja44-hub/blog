Addis Crown exists to make Ethiopian law and the policy decisions shaping ordinary life legible to the people they actually affect — without the density of a statute or the vagueness of a headline.

## What this is not

This is not legal advice, and it is not commentary built to provoke a reaction. Every piece here starts from a primary source — a proclamation, a court decision, a published policy — and works outward from there.

## What to expect

Expect plain language, cited sources, and a willingness to sit with genuine complexity rather than flatten it. If a question doesn't have a clean answer, that will be the honest answer we give.
